Introduction to Uniform Circular Motion

Motion with constant speed in a circular path.

1. Angular Variables

While linear variables $(s, v, a)$ describe motion along the path, angular variables describe the rotation.

  • Angular Displacement (\(\theta\)): Angle swept at the center.
  • Angular Velocity (\(\omega\)): rate of change of \(\theta\). (\(\omega = v/R\))

2. Centripetal Acceleration

In uniform circular motion, speed is constant but direction is perpetually changing. This requires a center-seeking acceleration.

\[ a_c = \frac{v^2}{R} = \omega^2 R \]
This acceleration always points toward the center of the circle and is perpendicular to the velocity vector.

3. Time Period and Frequency

Time Period ($T$): Time for one complete revolution. \(T = 2\pi/\omega = 2\pi R / v\).
Frequency ($f$): Number of revolutions per second. \(f = 1/T\).
