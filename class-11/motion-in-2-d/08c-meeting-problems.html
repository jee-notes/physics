Meeting Problems

Same co-ordinate at same time.

What is Meeting?

It's 2:00 PM in the afternoon and you call your friend and ask him to meet you at 5:00 PM near the fruit juice shop.

What does "meet" mean kinematically?

Two particles meet when they occupy the same position at the same time.

\[ \boxed{\vec r_1(t)=\vec r_2(t)} \]

Thus, meeting is not simply about the particles having the same position at some point in space. That position must be reached by both particles at the same instant.

For example, if one particle passes through a point at \(4:00\) PM and another passes through the same point at \(5:00\) PM, they do not meet there.

Meeting condition:
Same position + same time. \[ \boxed{\vec r_1(t)=\vec r_2(t)} \] The time \(t\) is common to both particles.
