Relative Motion in a Plane

Analyzing motion from a moving frame of reference.

1. Vector Formulation

The velocity of object $A$ relative to object $B$ is the vector difference of their velocities relative to the ground.

\[ \vec{v}_{A/B} = \vec{v}_A - \vec{v}_B \]

2. Rain-Man Problems

If rain falls vertically with velocity $\vec{v}_r$ and a man moves horizontally with velocity $\vec{v}_m$, the velocity of rain relative to the man is:

\[ \vec{v}_{r/m} = \vec{v}_r - \vec{v}_m \]

The man must hold his umbrella at an angle $\theta$ such that it aligns with $\vec{v}_{r/m}$.

3. River-Boat Problems

A boat crossing a river with current velocity $\vec{v}_w$ and boat velocity in still water $\vec{v}_{b/w}$:

\[ \vec{v}_b = \vec{v}_{b/w} + \vec{v}_w \]
  • Shortest Path: The boat must aim upstream to counteract the drift.
  • Shortest Time: The boat should aim straight across the river.
