Circular Kinematics: Angular Variables

Defining the geometry and motion parameters of rotational paths.

1. Angular Displacement (\(\theta\))

Angular displacement is the angle (in radians) through which a particle moves along a circular path. It is a vector quantity (for small values) directed along the axis of rotation (Right-Hand Thumb Rule).

\[ \Delta \theta = \frac{\text{Arc Length (s)}}{\text{Radius (R)}} \]

2. Angular Velocity (\(\omega\))

The rate of change of angular displacement.

\[ \omega = \frac{d\theta}{dt} \]

Relation to linear velocity (\(v\)):

\[ v = R\omega \quad \text{or} \quad \vec{v} = \vec{\omega} \times \vec{r} \]

3. Angular Acceleration (\(\alpha\))

The rate of change of angular velocity.

\[ \alpha = \frac{d\omega}{dt} = \frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2} \]

Relation to tangential acceleration (\(a_t\)):

\[ a_t = R\alpha \]
