Dynamics and Centripetal Force

The real forces that maintain curved trajectories.

1. What is Centripetal Force?

Centripetal force is not a basic force of nature (like gravity or electromagnetism). Instead, it is a role played by any physical force that acts toward the center of a circular path.

\[ F_{net} = m \frac{v^2}{R} \]

2. Real-World Agents

  • Tension: A string pulling a stone in a circle.
  • Friction: Tires of a car turning on a level road.
  • Gravity: The sun's pull keeping planets in orbit.
  • Normal Force: The walls of a 'Death Well' pushing on a motorcycle.

3. Horizontal Circular Motion

For a particle moving in a horizontal circle constrained by a string (Conical Pendulum):

\[ T \cos \theta = mg \] \[ T \sin \theta = \frac{mv^2}{R} \] \[ \implies \tan \theta = \frac{v^2}{Rg} \]
In horizontal circular motion, gravity is balanced by a component of another force (like tension), while a different component provides the required inward pull.
