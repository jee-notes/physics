Practical: Micrometer Screw Gauge

High-precision measurement of thin objects like wires and sheets.

1. Pitch and Least Count

The Pitch is the distance moved by the screw on the linear scale in one complete rotation of the circular head.

\[ \text{Least Count (LC)} = \frac{\text{Pitch}}{\text{Total number of divisions on circular scale}} \]

For a standard screw gauge (Pitch = 1mm, Divisions = 100), LC = 0.01 mm or 0.001 cm.

2. Taking a Reading

The total reading consists of the Pitch Scale Reading (PSR) and the Circular Scale Reading (CSR).

\[ \text{Total Reading} = \text{PSR} + (\text{CSR} \times LC) \]

3. Zero Error

When the anvil and spindle are in contact, if the zero of the circular scale does not coincide with the reference line of the main scale:

  • Positive Zero Error: Zero of circular scale is below the reference line. (Subtract it).
  • Negative Zero Error: Zero of circular scale is above the reference line. (Add its magnitude).
Remember: While Vernier Calipers have a resolution of 0.1 mm, the Screw Gauge is ten times more precise with a resolution of 0.01 mm.
