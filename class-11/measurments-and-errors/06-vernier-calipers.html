Practical: Vernier Calipers

Precision measurement of length, diameter, and depth.

1. Least Count (LC)

The Least Count is the minimum measurement that can be taken accurately with the instrument.

\[ LC = 1 \text{ MSD} - 1 \text{ VSD} \] \[ LC = 1 \text{ MSD} \times \left( 1 - \frac{n}{m} \right) \]

Where \(n\) VSD coincides with \(m\) MSD. For a standard instrument, LC = 0.01 cm or 0.1 mm.

2. Taking a Reading

The total reading consists of the Main Scale Reading (MSR) and the Vernier Scale Reading (VSR).

\[ \text{Total Reading} = \text{MSR} + (\text{VSR} \times LC) \]

3. Zero Error

When the jaws are closed, if the zero of the vernier scale does not coincide with the zero of the main scale, there is a zero error.

  • Positive Zero Error: Vernier zero is to the right of main scale zero. (Subtract it from reading).
  • Negative Zero Error: Vernier zero is to the left of main scale zero. (Add its magnitude to reading).
