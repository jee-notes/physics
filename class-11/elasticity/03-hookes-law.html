3. Hooke's Law

Proportionality between stress and strain within the elastic limit.

Hooke’s Law

Within the elastic limit, stress is directly proportional to strain.

\[ \text{Stress} \propto \text{Strain} \]
\[ \text{Stress} = E \times \text{Strain} \]

Where \(E\) is the modulus of elasticity.

Hooke’s law is an empirical linear approximation, not a universal truth for all materials.
