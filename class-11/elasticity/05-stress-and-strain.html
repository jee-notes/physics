2. Stress and Strain

Internal forces and resulting dimensional changes.

Stress and Strain

Stress
  • Internal restoring force per unit area
  • Cause of deformation
Strain
  • Fractional change in dimension
  • Effect of stress
Normal Stress: \[ \sigma = \frac{F}{A} \]
Longitudinal Strain: \[ \varepsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L} \]
Stress has units; strain does not.

Stress and Strain

Stress and strain are both defined at a point. In higher physics, stress is a tensor, but for 12th, we only have uniaxial stress.

Either stress is longitudinal or it is shear.

Thus, at each point, longitudinal stress is defined and shear stress is defined.

Both together provide complete information and are treated as scalars. Its a mathematical trick. Its like trying to define velocity in any direction as speed in x direction and speed in y direction.
