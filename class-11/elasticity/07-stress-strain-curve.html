7. Stress–Strain Curve

Graphical representation of a material's response to stress.

Stress–Strain Curve

  • Proportional limit: The region where Hooke's Law is obeyed.
  • Elastic limit (Yield point): The maximum stress a material can withstand without permanent deformation.
  • Breaking point (Fracture point): The point where the material fails or breaks.
Ductility and brittleness are read from the stress–strain curve.

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing elasticity with strength
  • Applying Hooke’s law beyond elastic limit
  • Forgetting sign convention in Poisson’s ratio
