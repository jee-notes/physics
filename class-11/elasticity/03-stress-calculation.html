Stress (Calculation)

How to calculate stress based on definition.

Stress Tensor

In general, stress is a second-order tensor. However, for our level, we calculate stress using its definition as force per unit area.

Stress depends on the orientation of the surface on which internal forces act. Based on this, we classify stress into:

  • Longitudinal stress : If the deforming force is applied along some linear dimension of a body, the corresponding stress is called Longitudinal Stress or Tensile Stress. So, when an object is one dimensional then we use the concept of longitudinal stress.
  • Shear stress : If the force is applied tangentially to one face of a rectangular body, keeping the other face fixed, the stress is called Tangential or Shearing stress. So, tangential stress is defined as the restoring force per unit area tangential to the surface of the body.

Here force F is exerteded on area A at angle \(\theta\) with the surface. For stress, this force should be internal force.

Resolve Force with respect to orientation of the area.

Force along normal \(F_n = F \sin \theta\) and force along tangent \(F_t = F \cos \theta\).

Longitudinal Stress: \[ \frac{F_n}{A} = \frac{F \sin \theta}{A} \]

Shear Stress: \[ \frac{F_t}{A} = \frac{F \cos \theta}{A} \]
