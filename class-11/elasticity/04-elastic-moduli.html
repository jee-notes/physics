4. Elastic Moduli

Quantifying the stiffness of materials under different types of stress.

Elastic Moduli

Young’s Modulus

\[ Y = \frac{\text{longitudinal stress}}{\text{longitudinal strain}} \]

Bulk Modulus

\[ K = \frac{\text{volume stress}}{\text{volume strain}} \]

Shear Modulus

\[ G = \frac{\text{shear stress}}{\text{shear strain}} \]
Higher modulus → greater stiffness,
not greater strength.
