Deformation and Elasticity

Understanding the geometric changes and material properties.

Deformation

Form is shape and size together. e.g. a circle of radius R has shape circle and size R.

Deformation is the change in either shape or size of the material body under the action of external forces.

  • Change in length
  • Change in area
  • Change in volume
  • Change in shape
Deformation is a geometric change;
But modulus of elasticity is a property of material only.
Just like resistance and resistivity.

Elasticity

Elasticity is the ability of a material to regain its original shape and size after removal of deforming forces.

  • Perfectly elastic bodies are idealizations
  • Real materials have elastic limits
