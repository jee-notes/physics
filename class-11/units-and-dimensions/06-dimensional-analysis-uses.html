Uses of Dimensional Analysis

Practical applications of dimensions in solving physics problems.

Why Perform Dimensional Analysis?

Dimensions classify physical quantities according to their inherent nature.

Once the dimensions of a quantity are known, they can be used as a powerful tool for analysing physical equations and relationships.

This process is called Dimensional Analysis.

Dimensional homogeneity is a property of physical equations.
Dimensional analysis is the process of using dimensions as a problem-solving tool.

Checking Physical Equations

One of the most important applications of dimensional analysis is checking the dimensional consistency of physical equations.

This is done by finding the dimensions of all terms appearing in the equation and verifying that quantities being added, subtracted or equated possess identical dimensions.

If the dimensions of the terms do not match, the equation cannot represent a valid physical relationship.

Dimensional analysis can be used to reject incorrect equations, but it cannot prove that an equation is correct.
A dimensionally correct equation may still be physically incorrect because of missing numerical factors, dimensionless constants, or an incomplete physical model.
However, a dimensionally inconsistent equation is always physically wrong.

Illustration 1:

A boomerang has mass \(m\), surface area \(A\), radius of curvature of its lower surface \(r\), and is moving with velocity \(v\) in air of density \(\rho\). The resistive force acting on it should be:

  1. \[ F = \frac{2\rho vA}{r^2} \log\left(\frac{\rho m}{\pi Ar}\right) \]
  2. \[ F = \frac{2\rho vA}{r} \log\left(\frac{\rho A}{\pi m}\right) \]
  3. \[ F = 2\rho v^2A \log\left(\frac{\rho Ar}{\pi m}\right) \]
  4. \[ F = \frac{2\rho vA}{r^2} \log\left(\frac{\rho Ar}{\pi m}\right) \]

Solution:

In objective examinations, an important skill is recognising when a problem can be attacked using dimensional analysis.

In this problem, the first hint is that, we have never encountered a formula for the resistive force acting on a boomerang. Then the main hint is that, the four options contain four different mathematical expressions.

These hints suggest that the problem is testing our understanding of dimensions rather than our knowledge of fluid mechanics.

The correct option should therefore be the expression that is dimensionally consistent. To determine this, we apply two simple checks:

  • The overall expression must have the dimensions of force, \([MLT^{-2}]\).
  • Since logarithmic functions can only accept dimensionless arguments, the quantity inside the logarithm must have dimensions \([M^0L^0T^0]\) .

Among the four options, only Option (3) satisfies both requirements. Therefore, it is the only dimensionally acceptable expression for the resistive force.

Dimensional analysis is particularly useful in objective examinations.
It can quickly eliminate options that violate dimensional homogeneity or contain transcendental functions with dimensional arguments.

Converting Units

Dimensions provide a systematic way to convert a measurement from one system of units (like SI) to another (like CGS).

\[ n_2 \times u_2 = n_1 \times u_1 \]

Deriving Relations

If we know which factors a physical quantity depends on, we can use dimensional analysis to derive the relationship between them.

\[ T \propto m^a l^b g^c \] \[ [T^1] = [M^a L^b (LT^{-2})^c] \] \[ \implies T = K \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}} \]
