Principle of Dimensional Homogeneity

The rule of consistency in physical equations.

1. The Fundamental Rule

The Principle of Dimensional Homogeneity states that only physical quantities of the same dimensions can be added, subtracted, or equated.

You can add 5 meters to 10 meters, but you cannot add 5 meters to 10 kilograms.

2. Mathematical Expression

In any physical equation of the form \(A + B = C\):

\[ [A] = [B] = [C] \]

This principle is used to check the correctness of physical equations.

3. Transcendental Functions

The arguments of mathematical functions like \(\sin(\theta)\), \(\cos(\theta)\), \(e^x\), and \(\log(x)\) must always be dimensionless.

In \(y = A \sin(kt - \omega x)\):
\([kt] = [M^0L^0T^0]\)
\([\omega x] = [M^0L^0T^0]\)
