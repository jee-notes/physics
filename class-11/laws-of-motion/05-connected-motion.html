Connected Bodies and Pulley Systems

Analyzing systems of multiple interacting objects.

1. Tension in Strings

Tension is a pulling force exerted by each end of a string or cable. For an ideal (massless and inextensible) string, the tension is uniform throughout its length.

2. Common Systems

A. Bodies in Contact

When two blocks are pushed together by an external force, they move with a common acceleration \(a\). The contact force between them can be biological or accidental, but follows the third law.

\[ a = \frac{F_{net}}{\Sigma m} \]

B. Simple Pulley (Atwood Machine)

A massless string passing over a frictionless, massless pulley connects two masses \(m_1\) and \(m_2\).

\[ a = \frac{|m_1 - m_2|g}{m_1 + m_2}, \quad T = \frac{2m_1 m_2 g}{m_1 + m_2} \]

3. General Strategy

For complex systems:

  1. Treat the whole system as one to find acceleration if all part move with same \(a\).
  2. Draw separate FBDs for each mass to find internal forces like Tension or Normal force.
