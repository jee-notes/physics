Algebra & Binomial Approximations

Simplifying complex expressions for physical analysis.

1. Quadratic Equations

Quadratic equations frequently appear in Kinematics, especially when solving the displacement-time relationship \(s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2\).

\[ ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \implies x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \]
In physics, we often must choose only one of the two roots based on physical constraints (e.g., time \(t\) cannot be negative).

2. Binomial Approximation

This is one of the most powerful tools for JEE physics. It allows us to simplify expressions involving powers when one term is much smaller than the other.

\[ (1 + x)^n \approx 1 + nx \quad \text{for } |x| \ll 1 \]

Applications:

  • Gravitation: Finding variation of \(g\) at height \(h\) where \(h \ll R_e\).
  • Thermal Expansion: Calculating changes in area and volume from linear expansion.
  • Error Analysis: Propagating small fractional errors in compound quantities.

3. Logarithms and Exponents

Wait useful for Radioactive Decay (\(N = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}\)) and Thermodynamics.

\[ \ln(x) = \log_e(x) \approx 2.303 \log_{10}(x) \]
