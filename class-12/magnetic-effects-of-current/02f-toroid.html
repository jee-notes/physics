Magnetic Field due to a Toroid

A continuous closed coil carrying steady current resulting in zero leakage flux.

The Toroid

A toroid can be thought of as a very long solenoid bent into a closed ring (a doughnut shape) so that its ends join. It consists of a large number of turns of wire wrapped uniformly around a circular core.

If the mean radius of the toroid is \( r \), it has \( N \) total turns, and carries a steady current \( I \), the magnetic field inside the toroid is found using Ampère's Circuital Law.

We construct a circular Amperian loop of radius \( r \) exactly spanning the interior of the toroid's windings. By symmetry, the magnetic field \( \vec{B} \) is tangent to this loop everywhere and constant in magnitude.

Applying Ampère's Circuital Law:

\[ \oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{l} = \mu_0 I_{\text{enclosed}} \] \[ B \cdot 2\pi r = \mu_0 N I \] \[ \boxed{B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{2\pi r}} \]

If we designate \( n = \frac{N}{2\pi r} \) as the number of turns per unit length along the mean circumference, the field takes the familiar solenoid form:

\[ \boxed{B = \mu_0 n I} \]

Key properties of the toroid's magnetic field:

  • Confined entirely inside the toroid — using Ampère's Law on loops in the empty central hole (\(r \lt R_{inner}\)) gives \( I_{\text{enc}} = 0 \), so \( B = 0 \). Outside the ring (\(r > R_{outer}\)), each loop encloses equal numbers of inward and outward currents, giving zero net enclosed current and thus \( B = 0 \).
  • Not perfectly uniform — since \( B \propto 1/r \), the field is slightly stronger near the inner edge and weaker near the outer edge.
  • Becomes uniform in the idealization of a tightly wound toroid where the cross-sectional width is very small compared to the radius \(r \).

Comparison: Solenoid vs Toroid

Property Solenoid Toroid
Shape Straight cylinder Closed ring (torus)
Field inside structure \( \mu_0 nI \) (uniform) \( \mu_0 nI \) (nearly uniform)
Field outside structure ~Zero (infinite case) Exactly zero everywhere
Field at ends \( \mu_0 nI / 2 \) No ends (closed loop)
Core Application Electromagnets, linear inductors Transformers, inductors with zero magnetic leakage flux
