Simple Microscope

The magnifying glass: utilizing a single convex lens.

Principle and Construction

A simple microscope is essentially a single convex (converging) lens of short focal length. It is also commonly known as a magnifying glass.

Principle: When an object is placed between the optical center and the principal focus of a convex lens, a virtual, erect, and magnified image is formed on the same side as the object. By holding the lens close to the eye, this enlarged virtual image acts as the object for the eye.

For comfortable viewing, the image should be formed between the near point (\( D \)) and infinity. The two extreme cases are image formation at \( D \) (maximum magnification, but strained eye) and image formation at infinity (normal adjustment, relaxed eye).

Magnifying Power (Normal Adjustment and Least Distance)

The magnifying power \( m \) is the ratio of the angle subtended by the image (\( \theta_i \)) to the angle subtended by the object when placed at \( D \) (\( \theta_o \)).

1. Image at Least Distance of Distinct Vision (\( v = -D \))

To get maximum magnification, the object is placed such that the virtual image forms exactly at the near point \( D \) of the eye. Using the lens formula \( \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f} \) with \( v = -D \) and \( u = -u \): \[ -\frac{1}{D} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f} \implies \frac{D}{u} = 1 + \frac{D}{f} \] Since linear magnification \( m_L = \frac{v}{u} = \frac{D}{u} \), the magnifying power is:

\[ m = 1 + \frac{D}{f} \] This is the maximum possible magnifying power for a given simple microscope.

2. Image at Infinity (Normal Adjustment, \( v = \infty \))

For the most relaxed viewing, the image should form at infinity. This requires placing the object exactly at the focal point (\( u = f \)). The angle subtended by the image is \( \theta_i = \frac{h}{f} \), and the angle for the object at \( D \) is \( \theta_o = \frac{h}{D} \).

\[ m = \frac{\theta_i}{\theta_o} = \frac{D}{f} \] This provides slightly less magnification than the first case, but the eye is completely relaxed.
