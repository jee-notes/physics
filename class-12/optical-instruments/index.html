Optical Instruments

Extending the reach of human vision through the manipulation of light.

Conceptual Overview

Optical instruments are devices that process light waves to enhance an image for viewing. The human eye is the most natural and fundamental optical instrument, but its capabilities are limited by resolution and light-gathering power. To overcome these limits, physicists and engineers use lenses and mirrors to construct devices like microscopes and telescopes.

The core principle behind all optical instruments is the manipulation of light rays using refraction (lenses) and reflection (mirrors). By carefully combining multiple optical elements, we can magnify microscopic details of cells or observe distant galaxies.

Ultimately, every optical instrument is designed to increase the visual angle subtended by an object at the eye, making the object appear larger and more detailed than it would to the unaided eye.
